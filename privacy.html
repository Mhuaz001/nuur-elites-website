Privacy Notice

Nuur Elites Learning and Consultancy · Last updated: 15 November 2026

Nuur Elites Learning and Consultancy respects the privacy of learners, parents, guardians, tutors and visitors. This notice explains what information may be collected through this website and how it is used.

Information we collect

When you submit a student application, we may collect the learner's name, date of birth, location, parent or guardian name, relationship, telephone number, email address, programme interests, preferred learning arrangement and additional information you choose to provide. Portal accounts may also be associated with course enrolments, lesson schedules, attendance, assessments, assignments and payment records.

When you contact us through WhatsApp or email links, the information you send is handled by the relevant messaging or email provider. The website's tutor-request and contact links open a prepared message and do not themselves save that message in our database.

How we use information

  • To process admission and tutoring enquiries.
  • To arrange classes, assign tutors and manage learner accounts.
  • To communicate with learners, parents and guardians about lessons and services.
  • To maintain attendance, assessment, assignment and payment records.
  • To protect the security and proper operation of our services.

Children and guardian submissions

Where information relates to a child, a parent or authorised guardian should submit the application or assist the child in doing so. Please provide only information necessary for the requested educational service.

Service providers and sharing

We use service providers to operate parts of the platform, including Supabase for database and account services and, when enabled, Paystack for payment processing. WhatsApp and email links may direct you to third-party services. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices. Nuur Elites does not sell personal information.

Security and retention

We use account access controls and database permissions intended to restrict learner information to authorised users. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. We retain information for as long as reasonably needed to provide services, maintain educational and financial records, meet applicable obligations and resolve disputes.

Your choices and requests

You may contact Nuur Elites to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable record-keeping and legal requirements. We may need to verify your identity and authority, particularly for information about a child.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email nuurelites@gmail.com or call 07030837054.

This notice is a practical website privacy statement and should be reviewed by a qualified Nigerian data-protection professional before production launch.
